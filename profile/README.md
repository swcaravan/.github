## About

Software Caravan is an independent studio based in Istanbul, Turkey. We design, build, and ship our own mobile apps and web products, with a focus on clean UX, solid engineering, and fast iteration.

## What we do

- **Mobile apps**: React Native (iOS & Android), published on the App Store and Google Play
- **Web**: modern websites and web apps
- **Developer tooling**: reusable kits and libraries we use across our own products

## Stack

`React Native` · `TypeScript` · `RevenueCat` · `Node.js` · `React`
